VPN Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions about choosing a VPN.

What is a VPN?

A VPN creates an encrypted connection between your device and a VPN server. This can help protect traffic from certain types of network observation and can mask your IP address from websites you visit.

Which VPN is best?

There is no single VPN that is perfect for everyone. Our current editorial ranking places Surfshark at #1 because of its combination of privacy features, usability, features and overall value.

Does a VPN make me completely anonymous?

No. A VPN can improve privacy and protect traffic in certain situations, but it does not provide complete anonymity. Websites, accounts, cookies, browser fingerprinting and other technologies can still identify users.
